# Map Legend
The pre-compiled maps are optimized for hiking and cycling.
Below is the legend for the rendered elements.

## Lines (Roads and Tracks)
* **Yellow line** — Paved road.
* **Gray line** — Unpaved road, trail, pedestrian, or bicycle path.
* **Orange line** — User route.

## Polygons (Areas)
* **Green** — Forest, park.
* **Light Green** — Industrial zone, commercial zone, scrub (base fill for most mapped areas).
* **Blue** — Water body (lake, river, reservoir).

## Icons (Points of Interest - POI)
* **Pink shield** — Police, fire station.
* **Pink cross** — Hospital, pharmacy.
* **Orange $** — Bank, ATM.

* **Orange cup** — Restaurant, cafe, food court.
* **Orange house** — Hotel, hostel, accommodation.
* **Orange triangle** — Campsite, shelter.

* **Orange basket** — General store, supermarket, market.
* **Orange t-shirt** — Clothing, shoe store.
* **Orange mountain** — Sports, outdoor equipment store.
* **Orange computer** — Electronics, mobile phone store.
* **Orange hammer** — Hardware, household goods store.
* **Two Orange circles** — Bicycle shop.

* **Pink hammer** — Workshop, auto service.
* **Two pink circles** — Bicycle repair workshop.
* **Orange rectangle with pump** — Gas/Petrol station.

* **Orange fortress** — Attraction, monument.
* **Blue fortress** — Waterfall, hot spring.
* **Orange rhombus** — Mountain pass.

* **Blue cup** — Drinking water, spring, well.
* **Two blue triangles** — Toilet.
* **Blue shower** — Shower.

* **Orange bus** — Bus station, railway station.
* **Blue bus** — Ferry terminal.
* **Blue airplane** — Airport.
* **Blue rhombus** — Ford (water crossing).
* **Pink diagonal cross** — Restricted access, forbidden passage, or physical barrier.
